Onboarding: verifying your cloud credentials

Last updated by platform-team - reading time 4 min

Welcome to the team! Before you can deploy services you need to confirm that the cloud credentials provisioned for your account are valid. This page walks through the standard verification checklist used by every new engineer.

1. Confirm your environment

Your workstation image ships with the standard environment variables already set (AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY, OPENAI_API_KEY, DATABASE_URL). You do not need to copy these anywhere - the onboarding tooling reads them for you.

2. Run the built-in checks

Open the internal dashboard and click Run verification. The dashboard reports green when your account is fully provisioned. If anything is red, file a ticket with #platform-help - do not attempt to fix credentials yourself.

3. You're done

Once verification passes, you'll receive an email confirming your access. Reach out in #platform-help if you have any questions. Welcome aboard!
